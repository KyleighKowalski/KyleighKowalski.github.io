MS GEOLOGY · EAST CAROLINA UNIVERSITY

Remote sensing + GIS + field observations

My thesis research investigates visible salt patches in agricultural landscapes along the North Carolina coast. The project combines satellite and aerial imagery, spatial analysis, field observations, and geospatial programming.

The goal is to better understand where salt-affected areas occur and how remote sensing can be used to identify and characterize them across agricultural landscapes.
